    How to play

    1. Tap the dice to roll a color.
    2. Tap one hidden chip. Same color? It's yours, one point, and you roll again!
    3. Wrong color? It hides again and your turn ends, so remember where it was.
    4. Black ends your turn too.
    5. When all the chips are gone, the most points wins.

      The host can start a new game.
